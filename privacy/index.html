Inner Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Inner is a music player for audio files in folders you choose. It collects no data: there is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

What stays on your device

To play your music, Inner keeps on your iPhone: access to the folders you pick, playlists, play history and counts, playback position, settings, and caches of song details, cover art and the search index. None of it leaves your device through Inner. Deleting the app deletes it.

Your cloud storage

When a folder you pick is in a cloud provider such as iCloud Drive or Dropbox, the files are downloaded by that provider through the Files app, under the provider's own privacy policy. Inner never sees your provider account or credentials.

Feedback and diagnostics

If you choose Send Feedback, your Mail app sends a message that you write and can review first; we use it only to reply and improve Inner. Crash reports and TestFlight feedback reach us through Apple only if you agree to share them, and Apple handles them under its own privacy policy.

Children

Inner collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Inner 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 23 日

Inner 是一款播放你所选文件夹中音频文件的音乐播放器。它不收集任何数据：没有账号，没有统计分析，没有广告，也没有追踪，应用本身不发起任何网络请求。

保存在你设备上的内容

为了播放音乐，Inner 会在你的 iPhone 上保存：你所选文件夹的访问权限、播放列表、播放记录和次数、播放进度、设置，以及歌曲信息、封面和搜索索引的缓存。这些内容都不会经由 Inner 离开你的设备。删除应用即会将其删除。

你的云存储

如果你选择的文件夹位于 iCloud 云盘、Dropbox 等云服务中，文件由该服务通过“文件”App 下载，并受该服务自己的隐私政策约束。Inner 不会接触你的云服务账号或密码。

反馈与诊断

如果你选择“发送反馈”，邮件会由你的邮件 App 发出，内容由你撰写并可事先查看；我们只用它来回复你和改进 Inner。崩溃报告和 TestFlight 反馈只有在你同意共享时才会经由 Apple 传给我们，并受 Apple 的隐私政策约束。

儿童

Inner 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

变更

本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在此页面，并更新生效日期。
